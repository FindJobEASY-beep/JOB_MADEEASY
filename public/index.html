Questions

When do I see the list?

Immediately after Razorpay confirms the payment, on this page. If the browser closes first, choose Already paid and enter the Payment ID from your SMS or email, plus the same email you paid with.

No. Companies change openings every day. Each row links to the company’s own page so you can see what is open right now.
