Questions

When do I see the list?

After the payment is confirmed, on this page. If the browser closes first, choose Already paid and enter the reference from the QR window, plus the same email.

What is in the list?

Direct job links, 250 companies with their job boards, openings that were checked, and apply portals. It is shown on this page. There is no Excel or PDF to download.

Are the jobs guaranteed to still be open?

No. Companies change openings every day. Each row links to the company’s own page so you can see what is open right now.

Can I share one payment with many people?

The list is meant for the person who paid, on this browser. There is no file to forward. Someone else would need their own payment.
